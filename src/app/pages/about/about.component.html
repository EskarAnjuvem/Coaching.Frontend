Academy of Physics | Sanjeev Kumar

General Physics

And a dash of Maths, Programming, Simulations

This website is meant to be a collection of things I have long wanted to build, or built but never documented properly.

Orignally, this was meant to host an app which will dynamically gather questions from a database based on user input. This would be helpful is taking tests with randomly collected questions, since most questions papers are often becoming repetitive. It has many other uses, which I will discuss when I complete the app.
This brings me to a pain point. It turned out that populating the database with questions and answers in LaTeX was no easy feat - its a time consuming and much lengthier process than expected. Still there is a glimmer of hope in form of automating the task. Six months hence, the project is still undergoing changes.

An easier job was to create a repository of schools and fetching them dynamically from a server database.
Also, I plan on introducing a page on different physical simulations I had created over last few years. This gives me the opportunity to talk about how Physics concepts, ranging from Calculus in Kinematics to Interference of Waves become clearer once you learn to program/code.

About Me

Passionate about Learning | Student & Mentor

Somewhere above or on the left, you would see a picture of two water jets from syringes merging into a single stream before breaking up into droplets. This is precisely the kind of thing that left me giggling as a child, and introduced me to physics and general sciences. The picture is almost a good practical representation of vector addition. As students keep learning, they discovers these practical applications of theory and gradually build/create stuff - sometimes new stuff - which can be a starting point of another discovery.

There is much more to Physics and Science than the mere facade of producing top rankers for IITJEE or NEET.
I have been mentoring students in undergradute physics for more than 13 years. This stems not from a desire to create IITians or ace the next competitve exam. Above all, I love Physics and Learning. My learning journey started from a childhood love of Physics and evolved to a more mature affection for Linear Algebra. It went on from the tech-savviness of Modern Web Development to a deep respect for Computer Architecture.

A student is always advised to keep learning, find joy in uncovering the machinations of natural and modern day world.
